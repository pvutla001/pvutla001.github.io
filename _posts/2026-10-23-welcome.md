A place for practical Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Power Platform, and PowerShell knowledge—written for people who build, administer, and improve these platforms.

## What I will share

- **PowerShell scripts:** reusable tools with prerequisites, parameters, examples, and clear explanations.
- **SharePoint and Microsoft 365:** administration, permissions, governance, migrations, and troubleshooting.
- **Power Platform:** application design, process automation, approvals, and reporting.
- **Lessons from delivery:** architecture choices, maintainability, and the small improvements that make technology easier to use.

## The approach

Start with the problem. Explain the decisions. Show the implementation. Document how to verify the result.

When publishing a script, I aim to include the context needed to adapt it: required permissions, supported tools, inputs, expected output, and limitations.
